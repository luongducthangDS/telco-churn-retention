# Decision Memo: Rescuing $139k Monthly Lost MRR via Onboarding & Tech Support Bundling

**To:** Chief Commercial Officer & VP of Customer Retention  
**From:** Data Analyst  
**Date:** October 2026  
**Subject:** Root causes of subscriber churn (26.5%) and targeted retention interventions  

---

## Executive Summary
Telco currently suffers a **26.54% churn rate** (1,869 lost subscribers), draining **$139,131 in monthly recurring revenue (MRR)**—representing **30.5% of total company MRR**. 

Contrary to conventional assumptions, churn is not primarily driven by high pricing; it is driven by two specific operational failure modes:
1. **The Month 0–6 Onboarding Cliff:** 50.8% of all churn events occur within the first 6 months of subscription.
2. **The Fiber Optic Paradox:** Fiber optic customers churn at an alarming **41.9%** (vs 18.9% for DSL). However, cross-variable isolation proves that when Fiber Optic is paired with **Tech Support**, churn drops dramatically from **49.4% down to 22.8%** (Logistic Odds Ratio = 0.53, $p < 0.001$).

---

## Key Quantitative Evidence

| Finding | Metric |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Total Subscribers & Churn Rate** | **7,043 accounts** $\to$ **1,869 churned (26.54%)** |
| **MRR Drain** | Total MRR: **$456,116** $\to$ Lost MRR: **$139,131/month** ($1.67M annualized) |
| **The Onboarding Cliff (Tenure $\le$ 6M)** | Churn rate is **53.1%** in months 0–6, dropping to **14.2%** after Year 2 |
| **Contract Risk Factor** | Month-to-month contracts have **2.90x higher odds of churn** ($42.7\%$ vs $11.3\%$ for 1-year and $2.8\%$ for 2-year) |
| **Fiber Optic + Tech Support Interaction** | Fiber Optic without TechSupport churns at **49.4%**; adding TechSupport slashes churn to **22.8%** |
| **Payment Friction** | Electronic check users churn at **45.3%** vs **15.2%** for Auto Credit Card |

---

## Action Plan & Financial Impact

1. **Complimentary 90-Day Tech Support for Fiber Optic (Immediate):**
   - Automatically bundle 90 days of free Tech Support with every new Fiber Optic activation.
   - Eliminates early installation and Wi-Fi configuration friction, the leading cause of early cancellations.
2. **Proactive Onboarding Care Program (Month 1):**
   - Deploy automated check-in SMS on Day 7 and outbound customer success calls on Day 21 for all Month-to-Month subscribers.
3. **Contract Lock-in & Auto-Pay Conversion Incentive (Quarter 1):**
   - Offer a $10 one-time bill credit for switching from Electronic Check to Credit Card Auto-Pay.
   - Offer month 13 free for converting from Month-to-Month to an Annual Agreement.

**Estimated Financial Impact:**  
Reducing month-to-month and fiber optic churn by just 15% retains **280 subscribers per month**, preserving **$20,800/month ($250,000 annually) in high-margin MRR**.
